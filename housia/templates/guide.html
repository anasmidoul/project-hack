    How it works

    What HOUSIA does, and how to try it.

    HOUSIA is an assistant for a landlord who is renting out a home. It answers the messages a listing receives on a rental portal, collects and checks each candidate’s document, runs the viewing calendar and its waiting list, and follows the chosen candidate to a signed contract.

    What HOUSIA does

    Eight parts of one rental, from the first message to the contract.

    {% call feature('f-replies', 1, 'Answers enquiries by itself', conv_href, 'conversations') %}It reads the messages a listing receives on a rental portal and replies from the listing’s own facts. A question the facts do not answer is not guessed: it goes to the landlord. Per property the landlord chooses “automatic” (replies are sent) or “assisted” (each reply waits for approval and can be edited).{% endcall %} {% call feature('f-privacy', 2, 'Protects personal data', cand_app, 'a document check') %}Before any text goes to the AI model, names, emails, phone numbers and identity or bank numbers are replaced by placeholders. The real values are put back afterwards, on the landlord’s side. You do not see the masking; you see its result, with real names, in the landlord’s pages.{% endcall %} {% call feature('f-link', 3, 'A personal link for every candidate', cand_page, 'a candidate’s page') %}No account needed. On their page candidates complete their details, submit the document the landlord asked for, and later book a viewing.{% endcall %} {% call feature('f-documents', 4, 'Checks documents', cand_app, 'an application') %}When a document arrives, the AI writes a short check for the landlord: what it is, whether it matches what was asked, whether the name matches. The landlord always decides.{% endcall %} {% call feature('f-ranking', 5, 'Ranks candidates', eval_href, 'the evaluation') %}By how many open viewing times fit each candidate’s availability; ties go to who applied first. The reason is shown. Assisted: the landlord picks who to invite. Automatic: the top candidates are invited, up to a limit. This is a simple, transparent rule, labelled as a simulation.{% endcall %} {% call feature('f-viewings', 6, 'Runs the viewing calendar and waiting list', relay_schedule, 'a schedule') %}Candidates book a time, or join the waiting list of a taken one. When a viewing is cancelled, the time is offered to the first person waiting. They have 30 minutes (or until the viewing starts) before it passes to the next.{% endcall %} {% call feature('f-messages', 7, 'Sends accept and decline messages', cand_app, 'the decision message') %}Written for the landlord, sent with one button, and shown on the candidate’s page.{% endcall %} {% call feature('f-contract', 8, 'Follows it to the contract', cand_contract, 'the contract') %}Attendance after the viewing, the final candidate, and the contract status up to “signed”.{% endcall %}

    What is real in this demo

    • Real: the AI replies and the AI document checks. They need the AI model to be set up on the server; without it, HOUSIA uses fixed template replies and skips the check.
    • Stand-in: the rental portal. “Portalia” is a small copy made for this demo. The same automation also drives a real portal inbox through a browser; that is not part of the public demo.
    • Fictional: all people and all documents.
    • Simulated: the ranking is a simple rule, labelled {{ simulation_badge() }}. The demo clock (“+10 min”, “+30 min”) moves time forward only inside the demo.
    • Background work: portal messages are read and answered by a separate process that checks about every 20 seconds, so replies are not instant.
